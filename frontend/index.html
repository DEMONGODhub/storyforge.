    Version history

      Export your book

      Keep your book safe on your computer
      1. Choose Story Forge book and press Download. Pick a folder you will remember. Next time, Ctrl+Shift+S saves to the same file.
      2. Turn your PC off whenever you like. The file stays on your disk.
      3. Later, open Story Forge and choose Import a book. Your text, chapters, scenes and graph come back exactly as you left them.

      The Markdown file restores your text, chapters and scenes. Only the Story Forge book also restores characters, events, relationships and suggestion history.
